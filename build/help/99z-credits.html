Credits and licences

The workbench is the Bioinformatics workbench of the Chronomic Medicine Group, cut down to what this project needs. It is built from open-source software and open data, each under its own licence. The full list, with the licence texts and the source code of the GPL programs, is in THIRD_PARTY.md and the folder assets/vendor/licenses/ of the site.

Data and services

  • The project's data (/data/examples/fnss): RefSeq proteins and the orthologue lists of NCBI Gene; TOGA's alignments of hundreds of mammal genomes (Kirilenko et al. 2023); the Phylogeny of Sleep database (McNamara et al. 2008; Capellini et al. 2008) and the msleep table of ggplot2 (from Savage and West 2007); the dated mammal tree of Upham et al. (2019); AlphaFold DB models (Varadi et al. 2024); gnomAD v4.1 (Guez et al. 2026; Chen et al. 2024); Genebass, the UK Biobank exome results (Karczewski et al. 2022); AlphaMissense (Cheng et al. 2023; CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 on the Zenodo record the predictions were taken from – DeepMind's repository now gives them as CC BY 4.0); and reads of a Brazilian porcupine from the DNA Zoo (SRA run SRR10331604). The project data lists every file, its source and the papers to cite.
  • The practice files next to it (reads/, variants/, annotation/, sequences/, plink2/) are those of the Bioinformatics workbench: the 1000 Genomes Project (sample NA12878), UCSC (hg19), Ensembl (GRCh37), NCBI GenBank, UniProt, and PLINK 2's own test files.
  • The Databases tab uses the public services of EMBL-EBI (UniProt, AlphaFold DB with Google DeepMind, InterPro, Ensembl, ENA, BLAST, Europe PMC), the RCSB PDB and NCBI (E-utilities). Cite the databases you use in your work.
