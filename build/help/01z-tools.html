Help with the tools

The guide's stages tell you what to do; these pages explain the tools you do it with – the terminal and its programs, the notebooks, R and Python, the Files tab, the databases, the 3D viewer and the rest. Come here when a stage uses something new, or search for a word with the box above.

Everything runs in your browser

The programs, Python and R run inside this web page, on your computer. Your files are kept in this browser (not on a server), so they are there next time on this computer and browser – download what you want to keep (My files → Download all my files, top right). Press Ctrl+\ to hide the guide and give the workbench the whole window.

This workbench is the group's Bioinformatics workbench, cut down to what the project needs: it has no Galaxy, Networks or Genome browser tab, and its terminal has only the programs you will use.
